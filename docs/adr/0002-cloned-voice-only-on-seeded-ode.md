# Cloned voice only on the Seeded Ode

Only the Seeded Ode is converted into the payer's cloned voice (stem split, speech-to-speech, remix), by a one-off script. Odes made in the app, including judges' Odes, keep Eleven Music's own vocals. This keeps the "that's my voice" moment while saving ~3 h of build, removing the split and conversion credits from every judge Ode, and keeping the Workflow to lyrics, compose, line windows and store. A real launch would need the conversion per Ode plus voice verification.
